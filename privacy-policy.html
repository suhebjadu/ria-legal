RIA AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

RIA AI ("RIA", "the app") is a personal AI assistant for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

1. Information the app handles

  • Account information. When you sign in, RIA uses Firebase Authentication (email and password, or Google sign-in). Your email address and a Firebase user ID identify your account.
  • Voice conversations. When you talk to RIA, your microphone audio is streamed directly from your device to Google's Gemini Live API so the assistant can listen and reply. Voice traffic goes to Google; it does not pass through RIA's own servers.
  • Search queries. When RIA looks something up for you, the query is sent to RIA's backend (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to the search provider (Tavily) and returns the results.
  • Profile and preferences. Settings such as your assistant name, voice choice and app preferences are stored on your device. Basic profile data linked to your account may be stored on RIA's backend so features like rooms and push notifications work.
  • Push notification token. RIA stores a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for your device so it can deliver notifications you opt into.
  • Screen automation and notification access (optional). If you enable them, RIA's accessibility service can perform actions on your screen at your request, and RIA can read incoming notifications (for example WhatsApp messages) to act on them. This processing happens on your device; the content is not uploaded to RIA's servers.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: app preferences, your Gemini API key, and automation data stay in the app's private storage.
  • Firebase (Google): account identity and authentication state for RIA's own Firebase project.
  • RIA backend (Cloudflare): account profile, room memberships and push tokens, linked to your Firebase user ID.

3. Third-party services

RIA relies on: Google Firebase (authentication, cloud messaging), Google Gemini Live (voice AI), Cloudflare (backend hosting), Tavily (web search), and GitHub (app update downloads). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. What RIA does not do

  • RIA does not sell your data.
  • RIA does not show third-party ads.
  • RIA does not upload your screen content, notifications or microphone audio to its own servers.

5. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account). This removes your Firebase account and your profile, rooms and push tokens from RIA's backend. Data stored only on your device is removed by uninstalling the app. If you cannot access the app, email us and we will delete your account for you.

6. Data retention

Account-linked data is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, server-side data is removed at that time. Diagnostic logs on the backend are kept for a short operational period only.

7. Children

RIA is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13.
